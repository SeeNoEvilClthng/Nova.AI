CUSTOMER POLICY

Billing, Cancellation & Refunds

Effective August 1, 2026

This policy describes how Nova.Ai digital subscriptions are delivered, renewed, canceled, and refunded. Paid checkout is currently safety-disabled; preview access does not create a charge.

Currency and billing cycle

Published plan prices are in United States dollars (USD) and are billed monthly when paid subscriptions are activated. Applicable taxes may be added during checkout. The complete price and renewal terms are shown before payment.

Digital delivery

Nova.Ai is a digital service with no physical shipping. Paid plan access begins after Stripe confirms successful payment and the subscription is synchronized to your Nova.Ai account. If access does not update, contact novaai.founder@gmail.com from your account email.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically each month until canceled. Renewal dates are displayed in Nova.Ai Billing and Stripe’s customer portal. Failed payments may pause paid features while saved company data remains available.

Cancellation

You can cancel through the Stripe customer portal from Nova.Ai Billing once paid subscriptions are active. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period, and access continues until then. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

First-time subscription charges may be eligible for a refund when requested within seven days of purchase and the account has used fewer than 10,000 AI tokens after payment. Renewal charges and substantially used subscription periods are generally non-refundable except where required by law. Duplicate, erroneous, or unauthorized charges will be reviewed promptly.

How to request help

Email novaai.founder@gmail.com from the address on your Nova.Ai account. Include the charge date, plan, and a short description, but never send full card details. We aim to acknowledge billing requests within three business days.

Plan changes

Upgrade, downgrade, prorating, and effective-date details will be shown in Stripe before a plan change is confirmed. Nova.Ai will not activate a paid plan change without the account holder’s action.

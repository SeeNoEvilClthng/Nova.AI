Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

This policy explains how Nova.Ai collects, uses, and protects information when you use the platform. Contact us at novaai.founder@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Information we collect

We may collect account details such as your email address; workspace content including company briefs, strategies, tasks, approvals, validation notes, pages, and leads; AI generation metadata such as model, token usage, and timestamps; and basic technical data needed to secure and operate the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, save and synchronize workspaces, generate requested AI work, operate approval and publishing workflows, provide support, prevent abuse, measure reliability, and administer subscriptions when billing is enabled.

Service providers

Nova.Ai uses service providers to operate the platform. These may include Supabase for authentication and data storage, Vercel for hosting and AI routing, AI model providers for requested generations, and Stripe for subscription billing. Providers process data under their own terms and privacy commitments.

AI processing

Information included in an AI request may be sent to the selected model provider to produce the requested result. Avoid entering secrets or highly sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for the task. Nova.Ai records generation metadata and preserves workspace results so work is not lost.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers required to operate Nova.Ai, when you direct us to publish or export information, to comply with law, or to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

Workspace information is retained while your account or company remains active and as reasonably needed for security, legal, and operational purposes. You can delete a company from Settings. For account-level deletion requests, contact us from your account email.

Security

We use access controls, authenticated workspace storage, row-level data protections, HTTPS, and server-side handling of private service credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can review and export workspace information, correct company information, control AI usage, delete companies, reset your password, and request account deletion. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights.

Children

Nova.Ai is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy as Nova.Ai evolves. We will revise the effective date and provide additional notice when required.

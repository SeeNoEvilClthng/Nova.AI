CUSTOMER POLICY

Shipping & Returns

Effective September 30, 2026

This policy applies to physical products sold by a merchant through Nova.Ai Reseller Studio. The merchant identified at checkout is responsible for the product, fulfillment, shipping, and returns. Nova.Ai provides software and does not take possession of merchandise unless the checkout page expressly says otherwise.

Order review

Before paying, review the product description, condition, size, quantity, price, merchant identity, and checkout terms. Product photos and descriptions must be reviewed by the merchant before publication. AI-assisted content may be used to prepare a listing but is not a substitute for the merchant’s verification.

Processing and delivery

Processing times, available destinations, carrier, shipping charges, and estimated delivery dates are shown by the merchant at checkout or in the order confirmation. Delivery dates are estimates and may be affected by carrier delays, weather, or address issues.

Fragrance shipping

Alcohol-based fragrances may be subject to carrier, ground-shipping, and destination restrictions. The merchant may cancel and refund an order when lawful delivery is unavailable. Customers are responsible for providing a complete, deliverable address.

Returns

For hygiene and product-integrity reasons, opened or unsealed fragrance and personal-care products are generally not returnable. A merchant may accept an unopened, unused product in its original packaging if the customer contacts the merchant within 14 days of delivery. The merchant’s checkout terms control where they provide equal or greater rights, and applicable law always applies.

Damaged, incorrect, or missing orders

Contact the merchant promptly after delivery and include the order number plus clear photos of the shipping package and product. Do not discard packaging until the claim is resolved. The merchant will review eligible claims and may replace, refund, or otherwise resolve the order as required by law and its checkout terms.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Banks and card issuers may require additional processing time after a refund is issued.

Contact

Use the merchant contact details in your checkout receipt for an order issue. For a Nova.Ai software or listing concern, email novaai.founder@gmail.com. Never send full payment-card details by email.
